About this chain. The initial sentence is invented; the three subsequent handoffs are deliberately staged examples, not messages passed blindly among separate witnesses. They show how an account can acquire details and apparent confidence without acquiring new evidence. No visitor data is collected, and nothing here establishes that a cup ever existed.
